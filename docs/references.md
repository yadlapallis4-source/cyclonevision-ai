#  CycloneVision AI — Research References

These references support the research foundation of the CycloneVision AI approach for tropical cyclone analysis using satellite imagery and deep learning.

---

## 1. DeepTrCy — Tropical Cyclone Life-Stage Identification

**DOI:** 10.1109/IGARSS52108.2023.10282363

**Relevance to CycloneVision AI:**

Demonstrates deep-learning-based identification of tropical cyclone life stages from satellite images.

#  Research Scope

These publications demonstrate individual research approaches relevant to tropical cyclone analysis.

They should not be interpreted as proof that the complete CycloneVision AI system has already been implemented or validated.

The proposed project integrates these research-backed concepts into a prototype focused on multi-source satellite cyclone-pattern analysis.
